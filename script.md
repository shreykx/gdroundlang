# gdroundlang - script

**what are npc characters?**

these are fixed characters that stay along the wall in this game, waiting for the player to interact. when the player comes close by they can answer by selecting them.

all characters:
* dungeon witch: a nasty old witch who was amongst the previous inhabitants of the cave and did chemistry for a living. her motto is to make you lost in her spell so you won't be able to answer those riddles and gain points.
* fire golem: asks you questions about strength and training, the descendants of the god of fire.
* retired sea turtle: answer its wisdom testing interrogations and it'll be impressed!
